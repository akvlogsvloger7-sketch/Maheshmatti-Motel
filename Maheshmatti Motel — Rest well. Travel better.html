Good to know

FREQUENTLY ASKED

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Reception is open 24×7. Standard check-in is from 12:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is possible on request.

Do I need to show an ID?

Yes. Every guest must carry a valid government photo ID (such as Aadhaar, driving licence or passport) at check-in.

Is parking available?

Yes, we have free, well-lit parking for cars, bikes and larger vehicles.

Do you serve food?

Our restaurant serves breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. Room service is available during restaurant hours.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, UPI and cards. Advance booking is confirmed on WhatsApp.
